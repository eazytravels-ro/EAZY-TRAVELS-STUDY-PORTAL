ℹ️ What You Should Know About These Programmes

  • Not every programme is a master's degree. Seneca awards an Ontario College Graduate Certificate. It is a recognised one year postgraduate diploma, but it is not a master's, and that distinction can matter for professional recognition and for further study later on. Northeastern in Toronto is the only Canadian institution offering a true master's (MS) in Regulatory Affairs, and Western is the only one offering a dedicated MSc in Drug Safety and Pharmacovigilance.
  • Some fees are charged per term, not per year. Western's CAD 14,694.42 and Saskatchewan's CAD 4,279 are per term figures across 3 terms, so the headline number is not the annual cost. Eazy Travels works out the true total with you before you decide.
  • Research master's programmes need a faculty supervisor. At Toronto, Saskatchewan, Manitoba and Alberta, a professor must agree to supervise you before your application is even reviewed, which can add months to the timeline. Eazy Travels handles that outreach as part of your application. UBC is more flexible and does not require a commitment up front.
  • Working in Canada after graduation. Eligibility for the Post-Graduation Work Permit depends on your programme's field of study, and the rules change. Eazy Travels checks every programme against the current IRCC requirements before recommending it to you.
  • Timelines for Nigerian applicants. A WES credential evaluation takes 4 to 8 weeks, and a study permit takes roughly 8 to 12 weeks, so the process needs to be planned several months ahead. Eazy Travels manages that schedule for you.
  • Tuition changes every year. All fees shown are the international student rate, checked against official university pages and fee schedules in July 2026. Where a figure is marked as subject to change, Eazy Travels reconfirms it with the university before you commit to anything.
